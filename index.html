FAQ

Questions.

What is Stonk Strategy?

The privacy layer for StonkFun launches. A one-time wallet launches your coin, the treasury pays for it, the ticker stays sealed until the block, there's no dev bag, and every copy of your ticker gets flagged on the vamp radar.

Is the private launcher live?

Private launches open with $STRAT. The vamp radar, the ticker check and seals work today.

What is a seal?

A SHA-256 fingerprint of your ticker, name and a random secret, made in your browser. It reveals nothing on its own. Post it, and when you launch, share the secret: anyone can check here that the seal matches, and the timestamp on your post proves you had the ticker first.

Where do the numbers come from?

StonkFun's public API, read live in your browser. Nothing on this page is simulated.

Is Stonk Strategy part of StonkFun?

No. Stonk Strategy is independent and built on StonkFun.

Does private mean the coin is hidden?

No. Once it's live the coin trades in public on StonkFun like any other. What stays private is who launched it.
